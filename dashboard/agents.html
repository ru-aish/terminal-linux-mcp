Operational contract

A local trigger is not a provider request.

Timers are durable. A restart does not erase a scheduled wake-up; the backend materializes it when due.

Completion is explicit. A gate opens only when the latest canonical assistant turn contains the exact configured marker.

Reasoning is enforced. Create, continue, wake-up, and completion-triggered prompts carry the configured high reasoning effort.

Synchronization is staged. Every tick performs zero or one physical call, and mutations are verified in later ticks.
